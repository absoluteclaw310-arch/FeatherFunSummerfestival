Privacy Notice

Your data is safe with us.

Information Collection

We only collect information that you voluntarily provide to us. This typically happens when you fill out our contact form or apply to become a sponsor. The information collected may include your name, email address, phone number, and business details.

How We Use Your Data

Any information you provide is used solely for the purpose of coordinating the Feathers & Fun Summer Festival. This includes:

  • Communicating with you regarding event details.
  • Processing sponsorship applications.
  • Answering questions via our contact form.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your data is used only for the administration of the festival and is not shared outside of the organizing committee except when required by law.

Your Rights

You have the right to request a copy of the data we have on file for you or to ask us to remove your information from our records. To do so, please contact us through the contact form on the homepage.
